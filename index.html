THE CHARTERHOLDER

How it works. You play a finance professional across a career: Junior Analyst (Level I), Portfolio Manager (Level II), then Chief Investment Officer (Level III) — difficulty rises like the real exams. Each turn is a scenario. Numerical questions give you fixed data and options — do the math yourself, then choose. Judgment calls have consequences that follow you. Every choice ends with a worked solution mapped to the CFA curriculum. Many scenarios are drawn from real financial history.
